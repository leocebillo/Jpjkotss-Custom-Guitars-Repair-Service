What we do

Three benches, one shop

Not sure what your instrument needs? Bring it in and we'll take a look and tell you before any work starts.

Bench 1

Custom guitars

A guitar built around how you play: body shape, woods, neck profile, pickups and finish, all chosen with you.

  • Electric & acoustic builds To order
  • Neck & body replacement Parts

Guitar repair

Setups and repairs for acoustic, electric and bass. Most players just need a good setup to make a guitar feel new again.

  • Full setup: action, relief & intonation Setup
  • Fret level, crown & polish; refrets Frets
  • Nut & saddle work Bone / synthetic
  • Electronics, jacks & pots Wiring
  • Cracks, bridges & headstock repair Structural
Bench 3

Orchestra repair

Repairs and adjustments for bowed string instruments, for students, school programs and working players.

  • Bridge fitting & replacement Setup
  • Soundpost setting Adjust
  • Open seams & crack repair Glue-up
  • Pegs, fine tuners & strings Fittings

How a repair goes

From drop-off to playing

You'll know the plan and the price before any work starts.

  1. Bring it in

    Drop your instrument at the shop in Weslaco. Call ahead so someone is there to meet you.

  2. Look it over

    We check it on the bench, tell you what it needs, and give you a quote.

  3. Bench work

    Once you say go, the repair or build gets done by hand, step by step.

  4. Pick up & play

    We call when it's ready. Play it in the shop before you take it home.
