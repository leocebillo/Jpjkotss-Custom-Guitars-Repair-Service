Hands-on, in-house

JPJKOTSS Custom Guitars & Repair Service is a hands-on luthier shop in Weslaco. We build custom guitars and repair the instruments people in the Rio Grande Valley play every day, from first guitars and gigging rigs to school orchestra strings.

Every job is done in-house with skilled luthier craftsmanship, and every instrument gets treated like it's the only one on the bench.
